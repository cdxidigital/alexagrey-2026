## Strategic position
**Fact (user-confirmed):** fleshsesh builds and maintains Ellie May's online identity and digital footprint, **not her in-person services**. She retains her own hours, rates, choices, client conversations, in-person bookings and payments. No physical-service referrals, worker recruitment or earnings commissions are permitted under this engagement.

**Product proposition:** creator-controlled premium web presence + consented publishing + privacy/takedown assistance + digital maintenance + jurisdiction/channel review. Differentiation is demonstrable creator autonomy and media provenance, not promises of increased personal appointments.

1. **Creator-owned assets:** domain, branding, accessible black/gold landing, local SEO where permitted, controlled publishing.

3. **Compliant messaging:** fact-checked copy, platform/jurisdiction review, approved contact destination, honest rates, precise price disclosures.
4. **Operations:** changes queue, explicit approval authority, incident response, backup and restoration, support-hours ledger, renewal reminder.
5. **Measurement:** aggregated B2B service P&L and creator satisfaction; do **not** track visitor sexual preferences, browsing histories or in-person conversions.

## KPIs (do not confuse creator earnings with fleshsesh revenue)
**North star:** realised monthly *fleshsesh* digital-service contribution per active creator account after documented direct costs.

- MRR = sum of active, invoiced recurring digital contracts, excluding GST.
- Setup revenue = completed, recognised digital builds, kept separate from MRR.
- Net cash receipts = collected cash net of refunds/fees (do not equate with invoices).
- Delivery contribution = recognised digital revenue - refunds/processor fees - tools/hosting - **fully loaded digital delivery labour**; fixed overhead and taxes separately.
- Monthly delivery hours, revised-hours overrun, contribution/hour, active creators, churn after complete paid periods, digital asset publication incidents.
- Creator-approved updates completed within agreed SLA (target to be agreed); rights withdrawals acted on promptly.
- No Ellie May personal earnings, physical bookings or site visitors' adult interests enter this P&L.
